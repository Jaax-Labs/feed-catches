Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.07962v1 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: TCSExam: Evaluating Frontier LLMs on the Full Range of TCS Research Authors: Yifan Luo , Zhengying Liu , Yang Yuan , Zhilin Yang , Andrew Chi-Chih Yao View a PDF of the paper titled TCSExam: Evaluating Frontier LLMs on the Full Range of TCS Research, by Yifan Luo and 4 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Can large language models (LLMs) advance theoretical computer science and resolve its long-standing open conjectures? To study progress toward this goal, we introduce TCSExam, a unified framework and benchmark covering the full range of TCS research. It evaluates theorem proving, solver engineering, and constructions alongside direct attempts to resolve open conjectures, capturing both concrete research accomplishments and progress toward long-standing research goals. TCSExam-Full contains 100 expert-reviewed tasks, with submissions checked by the Lean kernel or an executable task checker. TCSExam-Lite selects 20 tasks, five per track, reducing the number of task attempts by 80% at the same per-attempt budget. We evaluate eight agents on Full and nine on Lite. The TCSExam Index combines all four tracks: GPT-6 Astra leads Full (71.9), and Opus 5.5 leads Lite (72.3). Full and Lite rankings are strongly aligned across eight shared agents (Spearman's $\rho=0.93$), supporting Lite as a compact, discriminative benchmark. The evaluations yield improved solver outputs and mathematical constructions, together with independently verified special cases and bounds from open-conjecture attempts. TCSExam provides an extensible framework with task sources, Docker build recipes, and evaluation tools for reproducible use. Comments: 52 pages, 15 figures.
