Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.04792v1 (cs) [Submitted on 3 Nov 2026] Title: Density-Aware Reward Scaling: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Reward-Proportional Sampling Authors: Ria Shekhawat , Abhijeet Sinha , Yingtao Zhu , Dianbo Liu View a PDF of the paper titled Density-Aware Reward Scaling: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Reward-Proportional Sampling, by Ria Shekhawat and 3 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Reinforcement learning objectives that maximize expected return can concentrate probability mass on a small subset of high-reward outcomes, limiting diversity when multiple distinct solutions are desirable. We introduce Density-Aware Reward Scaling (DARS), a simple reward-processing mechanism that promotes reward-proportional sampling in continuous outcome spaces while retaining standard policy-gradient training. DARS estimates the local density of outcomes within each on-policy rollout group and inversely scales rewards by this density. The method requires no learned density model, auxiliary objective, critic, or modification to the underlying policy-gradient objective. In a controlled multimodal navigation task, DARS-GRPO avoids the single-mode collapse exhibited by GRPO and closely recovers the target mode allocation. Applied to text-to-image prompt adaptation, DARS-GRPO consistently improves image and prompt diversity across datasets, Policy LMs, and reward settings while maintaining good semantic alignment. These results show that density-aware reward scaling provides a lightweight way to improve coverage of diverse high-reward outcomes within existing reinforcement-learning pipelines.
