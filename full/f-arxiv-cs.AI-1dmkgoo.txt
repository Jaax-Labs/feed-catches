Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.20688v1 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: VIVID: A Visual Harness for Agentic Video Editing Authors: Wenhan Yu , Zhaoxi Zhang , Zhilong Tan , Weikang Li , Lei Sha View a PDF of the paper titled VIVID: A Visual Harness for Agentic Video Editing, by Wenhan Yu and 4 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Video editing requires agents to make editing decisions grounded in audiovisual observations and precise temporal localization, and to evaluate their effects in rendered outputs. General-purpose coding agents possess strong comprehension, reasoning, and code execution capabilities, while media interaction and evidence management mechanisms tailored to video tasks can further unlock their editing potential. We introduce VIVID, a visual harness that supports multiple native coding agents in video post-production without additional model training. VIVID provides tools for on-demand video inspection, audio analysis, and transcription, and preserves observations alongside their corresponding media versions and temporal coordinates. Agents can actively retrieve these observations as they reason, perform edits using their native coding and shell tools, and inspect rendered outputs to guide subsequent revisions. On AgenticVBench, comprising 100 tasks across Assembly, Repair, Sequencing, and Repurpose, Codex with VIVID achieves an average score of 58.6%, an improvement of 20.2 percentage points over native Codex. The same harness also integrates with Claude Code and OpenCode, achieving average scores of 55.1% and 37.1%, respectively, both substantially exceeding their native baselines. These results highlight VIVID's potential as a reusable visual harness for extending the capabilities of general-purpose coding agents to video editing.
