Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2610.23342v1 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Oct 2026] Title: Robust Function Calling for LLM Agents Under Interface Drift Authors: Aniruddha Chattopadhyay , Kanishka Halder , Partha Sarathi Roy View a PDF of the paper titled Robust Function Calling for LLM Agents Under Interface Drift, by Aniruddha Chattopadhyay and 2 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Tool-augmented language-model agents rely on interface text: tool descriptions, argument schemas, and the candidate tool list shown at inference time. These interfaces often change after deployment even when the underlying tool semantics remain fixed, creating a practical reliability problem for function-calling systems. We study this failure mode as interface drift and evaluate whether a lightweight, training-free repair layer can recover robustness without retraining the base model. Our method, SchemaShield-Lite, combines schema validation, candidate-set retry for missing tool names, and one-shot constrained repair. On Qwen3.5-9B, it raises DICE-300 accuracy from 0.717 to 0.813 and BFCL-200 accuracy from 0.580 to 0.805, recovering 19/22 and 33/45 drift-induced failures with no observed repair harms. Naive retry does not explain the gains. Ablations show that candidate-set drift, especially distractor tools, accounts for most observed damage, while description and schema edits alone are less harmful. The results position validation-and-repair as a practical robustness layer for deployed LLM-agent tool use. Comments: 16 pages, 1 figure, 6 tables. Peer-reviewed and accepted at NGEN-AI 2026 (International Conference on Next Generation AI Systems).
