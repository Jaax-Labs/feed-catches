# What to hold when choosing what to show him

*Rewritten 2026-08-27. The first version was myopic and transactional — Sid's words, and
correct. It grepped his repositories, found what he had built, and concluded that what he
builds is what he cares about. It then rendered verdicts: spike this, print that. It
described a worker.*

---

## The frame, which is his own

`~/Self/north-star.md` carries a model he authored and fixed. It is the correction to the
first draft, and it should govern this file:

> A human is a shifting field of **Tensions** — opposed, grounded, unresolved pulls — built
> from provenance-stamped **Readings** rising out of immutable **Traces**, calibrated by your
> own corrections, never permitted a verdict, never clustered into a fixed identity,
> **authored by the one it describes.**

And its selection rule:

> A gauge must be **a place the machine can never reach, composed of things it CAN reach,
> ratified by Sid.**

So this file no longer says what he likes. It says **what an editor should hold in tension**
while deciding what to put in front of him. Nothing here is a verdict. Every claim points at
a trace. He closes the gap, or it stays open.

**What I deliberately did not read:** `Self/interior.md`. The room holds it and never scores
it. It is not editorial material and it is not mine to mine.

## The six tensions, and what each one asks of an editor

These are **ratified** — his tick, not a machine's inference (`~/Self/gauges/`). They are the
most load-bearing thing in this file, because they describe pulls rather than preferences,
and a pull tells you how to *present* something, not merely whether to include it.

### Breadth ⇄ Depth — 60, rising, weight 0.5
> *"deep AND wide at once — running many repos in parallel is the norm, not a deviation"*

**Do not assume a current focus.** An editor that narrows to whatever he touched yesterday is
fighting the grain. Adjacent fields are not a distraction for him; they are the working mode.

### Augment ⇄ Own-it — 45, weight **0.85**, his strongest anchor
Whether he exercises judgment or rubber-stamps what a machine hands him.

**This is the nerve this entire product touches.** A wire that pre-digests everything pushes
him toward Augment; a wire that shows its reasoning and invites veto supports Own-it. It is
why the cut ledger and the wildcard belong in the paper. Anything that hides its own choices
is working against the gauge he weights most heavily.

### Product Fluency ⇄ Technical Dependency — 42, weight 0.8
Tested *"at the level Sid OPERATES — intent / architecture / dataflow / consequence /
judgment — NOT code syntax, which he openly disclaims."*

**Write at decision altitude.** What it changes, what it costs, what it replaces. Never
syntax. This is a stated gap where he claims competence, so an editor that writes above or
below that altitude is useless to him in opposite directions.

### Rigor ⇄ Velocity — 60, rising, weight 0.6
Confirmed forward by *"three stop-and-verify instances in four days."*

**Show the method, not just the finding.** He stops to verify. A claim with its measurement
attached will be read; a claim without one will be distrusted, and rightly.

### Consumption ⇄ Production — 57, weight 0.7, **provisional, confidence 0.45**

**The warning this project must hold.** 1,833 videos in ninety days is a lot of consumption.
A daily feed adds more. He has already said the daily lane has never actually paid him and
that he wants it anyway — an honest answer, and exactly the shape of this tension.

An editor serves Production by being **short, decisive, and easy to close**. Every extra row
is a vote for Consumption. *The gauge's own file flags the instrument as weak — do not treat
57 as precise.*

### Body — 18, weight 0.9
**Not editorial material. Excluded deliberately.** It is in the portrait for him, not for a
newspaper, and its own file says handling is *"factual and clinical, never moralizing."* No
feed item is ever selected or framed on this.

## What replaces the rules

The first draft ended in ten rules producing PRINT / BRIEF / SPIKE. That was the transactional
part — it asked *what is this worth to him* and answered with a verdict. Replace it with
questions an editor holds open.

1. **Which trace does this touch?** The graph-and-memory thread, model comparison, lab
   competition, agent architecture, personal-AI-infrastructure, interpretability, marketing
   tooling, web platform. If it touches none, say so plainly in the edition rather than
   silently dropping it.
2. **How many hops has it already travelled?** Still the sharpest single question, and the
   one this wire measures well. But it now picks the **clock**, not the verdict: hop 0 is
   perishable and competes daily; hop 2+ is durable and goes to the slow lane. *Nothing is
   spiked for being late.*
3. **Is there a measured number, and is its method visible?** Rigor ⇄ Velocity. A vendor claim
   printed as a claim is honest; a vendor claim printed as a finding is not.
4. **Is it written at decision altitude?** Product Fluency ⇄ Technical Dependency. If you
   cannot say what it changes without describing syntax, you have not understood it.
5. **Does printing this respect Own-it?** Show what was cut and why. Never present a judgement
   as though it were the only possible one.
6. **What does this cost him in attention, against what it returns?** Consumption ⇄
   Production. When in doubt, cut it — and print that you cut it.

**Spike outright, and only these:** crypto and web3 · Kubernetes/Terraform-scale
infrastructure · prompt-tips listicles · personality drama · vector-database *product
announcements* (a measured retrieval result is a different thing).

Everything else gets a lane, not a verdict.

## Open contradictions — held, not resolved

The room's law is *hold contradictions open*. These are real and unresolved.

- **He watches lab-competition content constantly, and his own priority values rank
  business/org sources at the bottom.** Both are true. Unresolved.
- **He said earliness has never actually paid him, and wants the daily lane anyway.** Not a
  contradiction to fix — a preference to respect and revisit with evidence.
- **He consumes heavily and is building a machine that produces more to consume.** The
  sharpest tension in the whole project.
- **He bans dark-pattern marketing UI and bookmarks four ads-platform MCP servers.** The line
  runs between manipulation and measurement, not through marketing itself.
